PART 1: THE WISHLIST

Total scope: 20 hrs/week across 5 pillars.

1. New Business Development — 8–10 hrs/week

This should be the biggest priority initially.

  • Build and continuously refine our prospect list
  • Research companies that fit the ideal-client profile
  • Identify the decision makers/CMOs/CEOs
  • Research each prospect enough to identify a reason to contact them
  • Draft personalized outreach for approval
  • Manage LinkedIn prospecting
  • Track referrals and introductions
  • Follow up with prospects who have gone quiet
  • Monitor RFPs and opportunities
  • Research potential strategic partners
  • Prepare Gail for prospect meetings
  • Keep the pipeline current
  • Schedule meetings
  • Maintain a simple "next action" system for every opportunity

2. Gail's Executive Assistant — 4–5 hrs/week

  • Manage calendar strategically
  • Prepare agendas for meetings
  • Gather background information before meetings
  • Create meeting briefs
  • Take meeting notes
  • Turn notes/ideas into action items
  • Track commitments made
  • Follow up with people on Gail's behalf
  • Draft emails
  • Organize inbox/priorities
  • Research things asked about
  • Prepare briefing documents
  • Create first drafts of presentations/proposals
  • Keep a running list of things to accomplish
  • Remind Gail about things before they become urgent

Goal: Become Gail's follow-through person. When Gail says "I need to get this done" — she shouldn't have to think about it again.

3. AI / Automation / Admin Manager — 3 hrs/week

  • Build reusable AI workflows
  • Create prompt libraries
  • Develop templates
  • Automate repetitive research
  • Automate prospect research
  • Create competitive intelligence reports
  • Create meeting-prep workflows
  • Turn meeting notes into action lists
  • Build proposal/document starting points
  • Create recurring reports
  • Organize agency knowledge
  • Document SOPs
  • Identify repetitive tasks across the agency that can be eliminated

4. Agency Operations — 2–3 hrs/week

  • Maintain a master task list
  • Track deadlines
  • Check that projects are moving
  • Coordinate internal follow-ups
  • Make sure people are doing what they committed to
  • Prepare weekly status summaries
  • Maintain SOP library
  • Organize agency documents
  • Track proposals
  • Track contracts
  • Keep client/contact information clean
  • Coordinate internal meetings
  • Identify things that are falling through the cracks

Note: This role is the air-traffic controller, not the person flying every plane. The existing team continues owning execution.

5. My Weekly CEO Dashboard — 1 hr/week

Every Monday, Gail receives a one-page dashboard with 5 sections:

  • NEW BUSINESS: Prospects added, contacts made, responses, meetings scheduled, opportunities, proposals outstanding, pipeline value, follow-ups due.
  • YOUR TIME: Meetings coming up, decisions to make, people to contact, commitments outstanding.
  • AGENCY: Important deadlines, projects needing attention, problems requiring escalation, opportunities, anything slipping.
  • AI/OPERATIONS: Automations created, processes improved, hours saved.
  • GAIL'S ATTENTION REQUIRED: Things Gail actually needs to deal with.

Days 1–30: BUILD THE MACHINE

Learn the agency, ideal client, services, voice, network, contacts, prospect criteria, calendar, workflows, and AI platform. Build the prospect database and operating system.

Days 31–60: RUN THE MACHINE

Run consistently: Prospecting, Researching, Following up, Scheduling, Preparing Gail, Managing action items, Taking administrative work off Gail's plate.

Days 61–90: OPTIMIZE THE MACHINE

Audit: What is producing results? What isn't? What can AI do instead? What is Gail still doing that she shouldn't be? What could this person take over next?

KPI: How many hours of Gail's time did we get back this week?

Frameworks to Build (In Order)

Framework 1 — Prospect OS (Week 1–2)

A structured database: company, contact, decision maker, reason to reach out, outreach status, next action, date. MAVIS populates research..

Framework 2 — Weekly Dashboard Template (Week 1)

Build the one-pager format once. MAVIS fills it every Monday from inputs or tracking docs.

Framework 3 — Outreach Prompt Library (Week 2–3)

Reusable prompts for: cold outreach, follow-up after no response, referral intro, RFP response.

Framework 4 — Meeting Prep Workflow (Week 2–3)

Standard brief format: who they are, what they do, why Cowley fits, conversation hooks, open questions.

Framework 5 — SOP Library (Ongoing, Days 31–60)

Document every repeatable process as it's built. Start with: prospect research, outreach approval, dashboard compilation, meeting prep.
