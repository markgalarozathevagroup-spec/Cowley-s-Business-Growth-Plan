Cowley Associates — Execution Guide & Daily Reports

Cowley Associates | MAVIS-Assisted Engagement | 20 hrs/week

PHASE 1 — Days 1–30: BUILD THE MACHINE

GOAL: Learn everything. Build the infrastructure. Don't prospect yet — set up so you can run.

1. New Business Development (9 hrs/week)

Steps to Achieve This Section:

  1. Week 1, Day 1–2: Schedule a 90-min deep-dive with Gail. Cover: ideal client profile, past wins, past losses, what makes Cowley different, Gail's voice and communication style, any active prospects.
  2. Day 2–3: Document the ideal client profile in a one-page brief: industry, company size, geography, decision-maker title, pain points, Cowley's fit. Get Gail's sign-off.
  3. Day 3–5: Audit existing contacts. Go through Gail's LinkedIn connections, past email contacts, any existing CRM. Categorize each as: warm (knows Cowley), cold (no relationship), or prospect (fits criteria).
  4. Day 5–7: Build the Prospect OS — a structured spreadsheet with columns: Company, Contact Name, Title, LinkedIn URL, Email (if known), Reason to Reach Out, Outreach Status, Next Action, Date Added, Notes.
  5. Week 2: Populate Prospect OS with first 30 researched companies. Each entry needs: what the company does, who the decision maker is, one specific reason Cowley would be a fit.
  6. Week 2: Draft 5 outreach templates (Cold intro, Warm intro, Referral intro, RFP response, Follow-up after no reply). Submit to Gail for approval and voice edits.
  7. Week 3: Set up RFP and opportunity monitoring — identify 3–5 sources (industry publications, government portals, LinkedIn groups) and create a simple weekly scan routine.
  8. Week 4: Identify 5 potential strategic partners (non-competing agencies, consultants, vendors who serve the same clients). Document in OS with a "partner outreach" tag.

End of Day 30 target: Prospect OS has 50+ researched companies. 5 outreach templates approved by Gail. Monitoring system running. Ready to start active prospecting on Day 31.

2. Executive Assistant (5 hrs/week)

Steps to Achieve This Section:

  1. Day 1: Connect Google Calendar (if not already). Get full view of Gail's recurring commitments: client calls, internal meetings, personal blocks, travel.
  2. Day 1–2: Build a "People & Context" file — who Gail interacts with regularly. For each: name, role, company, relationship type, what they typically need from Gail.
  3. Day 2–3: Set up the Commitment Tracker — a simple running doc where every commitment Gail makes (in meetings, emails, off the cuff) gets logged: what, to whom, by when.
  4. Week 1: "Shadow" Gail's workflow. Pay attention to: what she does repeatedly, what she forgets, what she asks others to remind her of. Document the patterns.
  5. Week 2: Build the Meeting Brief template: Who they are, What they do, How Cowley can help, Conversation hooks, Open questions. Run first brief for an upcoming prospect call.
  6. Week 2: Connect Gmail (if not already). Map the inbox: identify key senders, set up priority flags, understand what needs a response vs. what's FYI.
  7. Week 3: Draft first round of emails from Gail's rough notes or bullet points. Calibrate tone and voice based on Gail's edits.
  8. Week 4: Establish the daily rhythm: morning calendar preview, evening commitment check, flagging anything needing Gail's attention the next day.

End of Day 30 target: Commitment tracker running. Meeting brief template ready. Email drafting process tested and calibrated. Daily rhythm established.

3. AI / Automation (3 hrs/week)

Steps to Achieve This Section:

  1. Day 1–2: Inventory all tools connected to this engagement: MAVIS, Gmail (when live), Google Drive, any CRM or project tools. Document what each one can/can't do.
  2. Week 1: Build the Outreach Prompt Library — 5 prompts: Cold outreach, Warm intro, Referral request, Follow-up after no reply, RFP response. Each prompt has variable slots: [Prospect Name], [Company], [Specific reason for contact], [Cowley offer].
  3. Week 2: Build 3 document templates: (1) Prospect Research Brief, (2) Meeting Prep Brief, (3) Proposal Starter. Each should have fill-in sections MAVIS can populate.
  4. Week 2: Build the first SOP: "Prospect Research Process."
  5. Week 3: Build SOP #2: "Outreach Draft Process."
  6. Week 4: Build SOP #3: "Meeting Prep Process."
  7. Ongoing: Document every new repeatable task as an SOP the moment it's done for the second time.

End of Day 30 target: Prompt library built and tested. 3 document templates ready. 3 SOPs documented. All tools inventoried.

4. Agency Operations (2 hrs/week)

Steps to Achieve This Section:

  1. Day 1–3: Brief introduction to the existing team. Understand: what projects are active, who owns what, what's the team's existing communication rhythm.
  2. Day 3–5: Build the Master Task List — all current open projects, key milestones, owners, and deadlines in Google Drive.
  3. Week 1: Map the agency calendar: client deliverable dates for the next 60 days, billing cycles, recurring check-ins.
  4. Week 2: Draft the Weekly Status Report format — Projects in Progress, Deadlines This Week, At-Risk Items, Items Needing Gail's Decision.
  5. Week 2: Set up the Slippage Tracker — anything that was supposed to happen and didn't. Goes to Gail every Friday.
  6. Week 3–4: Run the first two weekly status reports. Calibrate format based on Gail's feedback.

End of Day 30 target: Master task list live. Status report format approved. Two reports delivered and iterated. Slippage tracker running.

5. CEO Dashboard (1 hr/week)

Steps to Achieve This Section:

  1. Day 1–3: Draft the Monday one-pager template — all 5 sections: New Business, Your Time, Agency, AI/Ops, Gail's Attention Required. Get Gail's sign-off.
  2. Week 1: Identify the data source for each field. Map what's manual vs. what will eventually automate.
  3. Week 1: Run Dashboard Version 1 — fill it manually with available data. Send Monday morning.
  4. Week 2–4: Iterate based on what Gail actually reads and acts on.

End of Day 30 target: Dashboard delivered every Monday for 4 consecutive weeks. Format finalized and approved.

PHASE 2 — Days 31–60: RUN THE MACHINE

GOAL: Execute consistently. The infrastructure is built — now use it every day.

1. New Business Development (9 hrs/week)

  1. Daily: Research 2–3 new prospects. Add to OS with full context. Target 10–15 new companies per week.
  2. Daily: Check follow-ups due in the OS. Draft follow-up notes for Gail's review.
  3. Weekly (Mon): Deliver "Prospect Batch" — 5–10 researched companies ready for outreach, with draft messages attached.
  4. Weekly (Fri): Update the pipeline: who responded, who's gone quiet, who to follow up, who to retire.
  5. Weekly: Scan RFP sources. Flag anything relevant with a 3-line summary and a recommendation (pursue / pass / monitor).
  6. Before every prospect call: Deliver a meeting brief within 24 hours of the invite landing on the calendar.

2. Executive Assistant (5 hrs/week)

  1. Every morning: Review Gail's calendar for the next 48 hours. Deliver briefs the evening before any prospect meeting.
  2. Daily: Triage Gail's inbox. Surface 3–5 priority items. Draft responses for routine ones.
  3. As requested: Turn Gail's bullet points or voice notes into polished email drafts within 2 hours.
  4. After every meeting: Deliver a clean action-item list within 24 hours: what was decided, who owns what, by when.
  5. Weekly (Mon): Send Gail a "Week Ahead" preview — key meetings, decisions to make, commitments due.
  6. Weekly (Fri): Review the commitment tracker — nudge overdue items, flag anything Gail is at risk of missing.

3. AI / Automation (3 hrs/week)

  1. Weekly: Run prompt library for all outreach, research, and document generation — no starting from scratch.
  2. Weekly: Document any new process that ran more than once. Add to SOP library.
  3. Bi-weekly: Pull a competitive intelligence report on 2–3 competitor firms.
  4. Monthly: Identify one manual task that could be automated. Build and document the solution.

4. Agency Operations (2 hrs/week)

  1. Weekly (Fri): Pull project status from the team. Compile and send to Gail by Friday EOD.
  2. Weekly: Review all deadlines for the next 2 weeks. Flag anything at risk 5+ days in advance.
  3. Daily: Monitor master task list for anything not moving in 3+ days. Coordinate nudge or flag to Gail.
  4. As needed: Prepare internal meeting agendas. Distribute action items within 24 hours.

5. CEO Dashboard (1 hr/week)

  1. Friday EOD: Collect all data inputs from OS, calendar, task list, AI activity log.
  2. Every Monday by 9am: Deliver the completed one-pager — all 5 sections filled, readable in under 3 minutes.

PHASE 3 — Days 61–90: OPTIMIZE THE MACHINE

GOAL: Audit what's working. Cut what isn't. Automate more. Increase Gail's leverage.

1. New Business Development (9 hrs/week)

  1. Week 9: Full pipeline audit: researched → contacted → responded → meeting → opportunity. Identify the conversion bottleneck.
  2. Week 9: Outreach template audit: which got the best response rates? Double down. Retire low performers.
  3. Week 10: Refine the ICP based on actual meeting data. Sharpen the targeting criteria.
  4. Week 10: Activate the partner/referral network — draft intro outreach to the 5 strategic partners from Phase 1.
  5. Week 11–12: Propose next-level automation — once CRM is connected, what auto-logs? What triggers a follow-up?

2. Executive Assistant (5 hrs/week)

  1. Week 9: Review commitment tracker: build response templates for the most recurring commitment types.
  2. Week 9: Identify any EA tasks Gail is still doing herself. Propose a handoff plan for each.
  3. Week 10: Build "Common Scenarios" email library — 10 situations Gail faces regularly, with a ready draft for each.
  4. Week 11: Optimize calendar: are meetings batched efficiently? Propose a better structure if not.
  5. Week 12: Identify 3 tasks that no longer need Gail's review. Propose moving to self-execute.

3. AI / Automation (3 hrs/week)

  1. Week 9: Prompt library audit — archive unused, refine active.
  2. Week 10: Identify top 3 manual tasks AI should be handling but isn't. Propose builds for each.
  3. Week 11: Build one new automation to eliminate a recurring manual task.
  4. Week 12: Update all SOPs to reflect how processes actually work now. Deliver final SOP library.

4. Agency Operations (2 hrs/week)

  1. Week 9: Review status report — cut noise, tighten to only what drives decisions.
  2. Week 10: Audit master task list — anything stalled 2+ weeks gets escalated.
  3. Week 11: Document any agency process still running on tribal knowledge.
  4. Week 12: Reduce manual check-ins — identify what the team can self-report.

5. CEO Dashboard (1 hr/week)

  1. Week 9: Review with Gail — which sections are most valuable? Which can be cut?
  2. Week 10: Identify all manually-entered data points. Map what integration would automate each.
  3. Week 11: Add any new metrics that emerged as meaningful during the Run phase.
  4. Week 12: Finalize dashboard format for ongoing use past Day 90.

Key Principle

"Don't bring me problems. Bring me solutions. Don't tell me what happened — tell me what we're doing about it."

Every report should feel like Gail is gaining information and confidence — not being asked to manage something she already delegated.
